01 The House

A wedding is one day.
Looking like yourself
is the whole point.

Prachi Bridal Hub — known around town simply as Prachi Collection — has stood opposite S.N. College in Vaikunth Nagar since 2014. Under one roof: the bride, the groom, and everyone standing beside them.

The racks turn over constantly, so what you see is what's being worn this season — trending bridal lehengas alongside traditional Rajasthani and Gujarati work, sherwanis, open jodhpuris, suits, blazers, and the full run of groom accessories. Take a piece for the day, or take it home for good.

04 Rent or Buy

Wear it once.
Or wear it always.

Every piece in the store can be taken either way. Most families rent the heavy occasion wear and buy the pieces they'll reach for again.

On Rent

  • The full bridal and groom range, including the heaviest work
  • Wedding jewellery included with the dress — no extra charge
  • Fitting adjusted in-store before the function
  • Ideal for outfits worn for a single ceremony

To Buy

  • Anything on the floor is available to purchase
  • Party wear, blazers and suits worth keeping
  • Alterations handled before you take it home
  • For the pieces that outlive the wedding

Pricing depends on the piece and the season — call 89894 23736 or drop us a message and we'll tell you straight away.
